# Client Service Request Desk

How we handle client emails that need work (certificates, changes, ID cards, billing, claims). This is **client files only** — not office admin.

## Where things live

- **Zoho Cases** — the pipeline (New → In progress → Waiting on carrier → Waiting on client → Done)
- **Client Service Desk** (Zoho Creator) — where you work the list
- **Amy** — Lamar asks her from the email; she creates the Case and the steps after he confirms

You do **not** copy the request into a second system. Check boxes in the desk; that updates Zoho.

## Open the desk

From the Cases board, open **Client Service Desk**. You should see your open items first. Toggle to all open if you are covering for someone.

Each row shows: client, policy number, type of request, due date, and the next step that is not done.

## Work a request

Click the row.

1. Read the client name, policy, and the email note at the top.
2. Do the next unchecked step. Check it off when it is actually done.
3. If you are waiting on the carrier or the client, use those buttons. Do not leave it sitting in In progress.
4. When every required step is done, complete the request. It moves to Done and shows on the completed list with the client and policy.

If a step is wrong or missing, add a short note on the request. Do not invent a second checklist somewhere else.

## What the steps usually look like

**Certificate:** confirm holder and extra wording → check the policy is active → issue → send → file.

**Change (car, driver, address, name):** confirm the change and date → check the policy → send to carrier → confirm it came back → tell the client → file.

**ID cards:** confirm who needs them → pull cards → send → file.

**Billing:** identify the bill → check status → reply to the client → note the file.

**Claim:** capture what happened → report to the carrier → send the claim number to the client → flag Lamar.

## Completed list

The completed tab is a running list: client, policy, type, date closed. Use it when someone asks "did we do the COI for X?"

## What not to put here

Office admin (subscriptions, website, internal portals) does not belong on this desk. If it is not a client policy file, leave it out.
