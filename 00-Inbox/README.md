# 00-Inbox

Unsorted captures, quick notes, and incoming items that haven't been filed yet.

## Purpose
This is the landing zone for everything that comes in before it gets properly categorized. Voice notes, quick thoughts, document drops, and anything captured on the go.

## Rules
- Process weekly: move items to their proper 01-05 folder
- If something has been here > 7 days, it either gets filed or archived
- Agents (Hermes, OpenClaw) can drop items here for human triage

## What Goes Here
- Quick capture notes
- Unprocessed documents
- Agent-generated drafts pending review
- Voice transcriptions
- Anything that needs sorting
